## Checks that passed

- 171 of 171 HTML pages return 200. No redirect chains in the sitemap.
- Every page has a title, a meta description and a self-referencing canonical.
- No noindex pages. No duplicate titles or descriptions.
- Every page carries JSON-LD, including BreadcrumbList.
- `http://` and the non-www host both redirect to `https://www.`. Unknown URLs return a true 404.
- `robots.txt` is clean and points to the sitemap index.
- Images flagged by a naive crawl as "missing alt" are decorative icons with a deliberate empty `alt=""`. That is correct, so it is not an issue. The four missing alt texts from the May Ahrefs audit still need the Ahrefs list to identify.
